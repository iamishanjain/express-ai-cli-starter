# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This is a short adaptation; the full Covenant text applies where this document is silent.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Public or private harassment, trolling, or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report violations privately by opening a [private security advisory](https://github.com/iamishanjain/express-ai-cli-starter/security/advisories/new) on this repository and marking it as a conduct report, or by contacting the repository owner (iamishanjain) through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially and promptly.
